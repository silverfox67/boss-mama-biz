Let’s be completely honest: you didn't start looking for an online business because you wanted a second full-time job.

The problem isn't your work ethic. The problem is that traditional online business models demand the one thing busy moms don't have: infinite time.

That’s where digital products and automated affiliate systems change the entire game. By leveraging smart tech instead of your limited hours, you create a system that works even when you're busy handling real life.

The Busy Mom’s Quick Guide to Automated Income

1. Is this just another MLM or pyramid scheme in disguise?
Absolutely not. With digital products and affiliate marketing, you are either selling your own assets or promoting established programs for a direct commission. There are no downlines to build, no monthly team quotas to hit, and no garage full of physical inventory you’re forced to buy. You keep your profits, and you own your business.
2. Do I need a massive social media following or a tech degree?
No, that is a total myth. You don’t need 100k followers to make an impact or a sale. Modern strategies focus on high-intent, targeted content that attracts people who are already looking for solutions. Plus, the systems used here run on pre-built tech stacks. If you can copy, paste, and follow a video tutorial, you have all the tech skills required.
3. How can this actually run "on autopilot" while I'm with my kids?
Through simple backend systems. Instead of sitting in your DMs all day manually pitching people, you use straightforward, automated tools (like pre-set text replies and automated emails). Your social media posts simply guide interested people to your website, where the system handles the information and secure checkout 24/7. The digital product delivers itself automatically straight to their inbox while you're at the grocery store, making dinner, or spending time with your family.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you get started.

Do I need a huge social media following to make money?
Absolutely not. Digital products and affiliate marketing work even with a small or zero following. The systems Kristan recommends focus on automated funnels, not viral content. You don't need 100,000 followers — you need the right offer in front of the right person.
Is this another MLM or pyramid scheme?
100% no. You are not recruiting anyone. You are not required to hit monthly purchase quotas. Affiliate marketing means you recommend a product, someone buys it, you earn a commission. No downlines. No monthly minimums. No pressure on your friends and family.
What is PLR and how does it work?
PLR (Private Label Rights) means you purchase a done-for-you digital product — like an ebook, template, or course — and you get the right to rebrand it as your own and resell it for 100% of the profit. You didn't create it, but you own the right to sell it. Use our free PLR Filter tool to find the right products for your niche.
Why use this site instead of just going to Stan Store directly?
Stan Store is just a link page. This site is a full business toolkit — an interactive income quiz, a potential calculator, a PLR filter, an AI income coach, and curated recommendations with full breakdowns. You get context, clarity, and Kristan's personal endorsement before you spend a single dollar.
How much time does this realistically take per day?
Like any real business, it takes effort to set up. In the beginning, you will need to invest time to learn the strategies and build your foundation. Once your systems are active, most creators spend 1-2 hours a day posting content and engaging with their audience, while simple automations handle the back-end details.
I'm not techy at all. Will this work for me?
Yes. Every offer on this site was selected partly because it's beginner-friendly. Stacked by Emily literally shows you the screen and walks you through it. The $27 Create & Sell guide is designed for someone who has never made a digital product before. If you can use your phone, you can do this.
